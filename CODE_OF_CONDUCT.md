# Code of Conduct

We want X-deepresearch to be a useful, respectful, and security-conscious open-source project.

## Expectations

- Treat contributors and users respectfully, regardless of background, experience, or viewpoint.
- Provide reproducible issue reports and engage with technical disagreements constructively.
- Respect privacy. Never publish another person's X cookies, private research results, personal information, or secrets.
- Do not use project discussions to promote harassment, targeted attacks, platform-access bypasses, or prohibited automation.
- Do not submit fake download statistics, security findings, or research results.

Project maintainers may edit, lock, or remove abusive material, and close contributions that violate these rules.

## Reporting

For ordinary conduct concerns, open an issue without sharing private data. For credential leaks, vulnerabilities, or other sensitive reports, use GitHub's **private vulnerability reporting** if enabled. Do **not** put sensitive details in public issues.

The maintainers will review reports when possible. There is currently no guaranteed response-time SLA.
